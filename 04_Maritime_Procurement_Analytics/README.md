Maritime Procurement Intelligence Dashboard
📊 Project Overview

This project presents a Maritime Procurement Intelligence Dashboard built using Power BI to analyze procurement performance across vendors, purchase orders, delivery, cost, quality, and supply risk.

The analysis uses 130 simulated purchase orders representing $7.13M in procurement spend.

🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze overall procurement spending
Measure purchase order delivery performance
Identify vendor-related delivery delays
Analyze vendor cost variance
Evaluate supplier quality and performance
Identify high-risk purchase orders
Support proactive vendor and procurement decision-making
📌 Key Metrics
Total Procurement Spend: $7.13M
Purchase Orders Analyzed: 130
On-Time Delivery: 44.62%
High-Risk Purchase Orders: 39
Average Vendor Performance Score: 82.7
Maximum Vendor Cost Variance: 10.7%
Average Vendor Delay: 8.4 days
🔍 Key Analysis
Vendor Performance

Analyzed supplier performance using vendor performance scores and operational metrics.

Delivery Performance

Measured on-time delivery and analyzed vendor delays to identify potential supply chain issues.

Cost Analysis

Analyzed procurement costs and vendor cost variance to identify areas requiring attention.

Quality Analysis

Compared supplier quality performance with overall vendor performance.

Supply Risk

Identified high-risk purchase orders to support proactive procurement and supplier risk management.

💡 Key Finding

The analysis showed that procurement performance should not be evaluated based on cost alone.

Delivery reliability, quality, vendor performance, and supply risk can also have significant operational implications.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of procurement KPIs and helps identify vendor performance issues, delivery delays, cost variances, quality concerns, and supply risks.

The analysis supports a shift from reactive procurement problem-solving toward proactive supplier and risk management.
